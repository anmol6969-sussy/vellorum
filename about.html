Our Heritage

A Legacy of Precision and Elegance.

Founded in the heart of Switzerland, Vellorum Watches was born from a singular vision: to create timepieces that are not merely instruments for telling time, but heirlooms that tell a story.

For over a century, our master watchmakers have dedicated their lives to the pursuit of perfection. Every Vellorum watch is a testament to this unwavering commitment, meticulously assembled by hand using only the finest materials—from scratch-resistant sapphire crystals to high-grade titanium and exquisite rose gold.

We believe that true luxury lies in the details. The intricate movement of our mechanical calibers, the flawless polish of our cases, and the seamless integration of classic design with modern innovation are what make a Vellorum watch unmistakable.

When you wear a Vellorum, you are not just wearing a watch; you are wearing a piece of history, a symbol of achievement, and a promise of enduring quality.
